Workshop 01 · Introduction

Meet Your AI Coworker

What AI can already do for your business

Don’t just ask AI questions. Give AI work.

  • 40 min or 2 hours two session formats
  • Live demos on real business data
  • Plain language no coding

The shift

Most of us use AI like a search box. A question goes in, an answer comes out, and then we do the work ourselves. This session shows the other way: hand AI a job, your information and what done looks like, and get back finished work.

Monday brief · Sep 28 – Oct 5 Real run · Oct 5, 2026

This arrived Monday at 6 AM. Nobody asked.

An AI job set up once for a café-bakery watched the local market, wrote a one-page brief and left drafts for the owner to approve.

  • A new bakery opened in Plano.
  • A new matcha café opened in Frisco.
  • Matcha lattes nearby run $6.27 to $8.78. Where it couldn’t find a price, it wrote “not listed” instead of guessing.

Three jobs you’ll see run live

  • MarketA weekly brief

    Competitors, prices and local news, with a source behind each claim.

  • CustomersA week of reviews

    What customers love, what frustrates them and what they ask, counted, not guessed.

  • SalesA job in the background

    Finds businesses that may need you and drafts the outreach. It contacts no one.

What you’ll learn

  • Six kinds of work AI can do todayAnswer and write, research and cite, work with your files, connect to your apps, take steps for you, run on a schedule.
  • Brief it like a new hireGive it the goal, your real information and what done looks like. Then give notes on the draft and ask it what’s weakest.
  • When AI makes things upWhy it sounds sure when it has nothing to go on, and the habits that catch it.
  • When to trust itAsk how sure it is, let the stakes decide, and approve before it acts.
